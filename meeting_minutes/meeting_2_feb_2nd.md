Questions
- How does alpha diversity of the microbiome differ between patients with CPP and CPP-endo? 
- How does alpha diversity of the microbiome differ between patients with healthy individuals and individuals with and CPP?
- How does beta diversity of the microbiome differ between patients with CPP and CPP-endo? 
- How does beta diversity of the microbiome differ between patients with healthy individuals and individuals with and CPP?
- Can species like (Strep, lacto, etc.) be used to make a predictive model to see how it can predict patient status at other sites?
- Can we perform taxonomic analysis to the species level for patients with CPP? 
- How is beta and alpha diversity different based on vaginal samples vs rectal samples?
